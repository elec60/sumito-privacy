Sumito Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Sumito has no accounts and no ads, and the game itself plays fully offline. It sends usage statistics and crash reports only if you agree. Apart from that, the app goes online only for the full unlock purchase and, on Android, for the AR mode.

Who is responsible

Mirhashem Mousavi Mehmandousti, the developer of Sumito, is the controller of your data in the sense of the GDPR. See "Contact" below.

Stored on your device

Your settings (theme, board, figures, sound, haptics, hints, move limit and the name you play nearby under), your answer to the question about usage statistics, your saved game and whether you own the full unlock are stored locally on your device. They may be included in your personal device backup (Android backup, iCloud or a computer backup) if you have backups enabled. Deleting the app removes them.

Playing nearby

"Play nearby" connects two devices directly, over Bluetooth or the local Wi-Fi network. The devices exchange the name you chose, the moves and the game state, and nothing else. No server is involved and nothing is kept after the game. On older Android versions the system asks for location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices; Sumito does not read or store your location.

Camera and AR

AR mode uses the camera to place the board on a real surface. The camera image is processed on your device; Sumito never records, stores or sends it.

On iOS, AR mode uses Apple's ARKit, which works on the device. On Android it runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. ARCore is installed and updated through Google Play and may send Google information about its own use; see how Google uses data.

Usage statistics and crash reports

On first launch Sumito asks whether it may send usage statistics and crash reports. Nothing is sent until you say yes, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings > Privacy. The Cafe Bazaar build of the app contains no such reporting and does not ask.

If you agree, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which processes the data on the developer's behalf. Sent are:

  • Analytics: app opens and how long the app is used; which screens are opened; when a game starts and ends, with its mode, computer level, board layout, result, how it ended and the number of moves. With this come the device model, operating system and app version, language, an approximate location (country or region) derived from the IP address, and a random ID that Firebase creates for this installation.
  • Crashlytics: when the app crashes, the crash details, the device model, the operating system and app version, the time and a random installation ID. A crash from before you agreed may stay on your device and be sent once you agree.

No name, email, account, advertising ID or precise location is sent, and the data is not used for advertising or shared with anyone for it. It tells the developer how the game is played, which computer levels are too hard or too easy, and what made the app crash.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for storing and reading the installation ID on your device, § 25(1) TDDDG). Withdrawing it stops all further sending; what was sent before stays lawful. Analytics data is kept for 14 months, crash reports for 90 days. See the Firebase privacy information.

Full unlock purchase

On Android, the full unlock is bought through Google Play or Cafe Bazaar. They process the payment under their own privacy policies; Sumito never sees your payment details and only learns whether the unlock is owned. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). On iOS everything is free and there is no purchase.

Recipients and transfers

Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC receive the data described above. Google may process it in the United States and other countries, relying on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses. Cafe Bazaar receives what it needs to process a purchase made through it.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority. The statistics and crash reports carry no name, and the developer cannot tell from them who you are; please include how we can find your data when you write to us. Apart from them, the developer holds no data about you: everything else stays on your device.

Children

Sumito does not ask for or collect anyone's name, contact details or account. If you are under 16, please say yes to usage statistics and crash reports only with a parent's or guardian's agreement; the game plays the same either way.
